Who we are

A research firm built around primary evidence.

Most market intelligence is recycled. Desk research, vendor reports and industry consensus describe what a market already believes — rarely what is actually happening inside it.

Consultancy Research International works the other way round. Every engagement begins with the commercial question, and the research is designed specifically to answer it — quantitative surveys, qualitative interviews, structured data collection and analysis of the underlying numbers. We test what we find against the published record, and hand back an evidence base that can be defended.

We are independent. We do not sell software, we take no position in the markets we study, and we are not paid by the companies we research. Our only product is the quality of the evidence.

How we work

A method designed to be audited.

Every engagement runs the same four stages. Every conclusion traces back to a source record a client can inspect. If we cannot evidence it, it does not go in the report.

Discuss a brief

  1. Frame the commercial question

    We convert a broad request into a written research question with defined decision thresholds, so both sides know what an answer looks like before work begins.

  2. Design the research and define the sample

    Method, sample and instrument are specified before any collection begins, so the evidence base is built to answer the question rather than assembled from whatever was easiest to reach.

  3. Collect and triangulate

    Quantitative and qualitative work run in parallel and are cross-checked against published data. Where sources disagree, we report the disagreement rather than averaging it away.

  4. Deliver with a clear line of sight

    Findings arrive as a defensible argument: the conclusion, the confidence attached to it, the evidence behind it, and what would need to be true for it to be wrong.
